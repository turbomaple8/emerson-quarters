When to Book Summer Intern Housing in Seattle: A Timeline From Offer to Move-In

2026-10-16 · Part of Summer Intern Housing in Seattle

Summer internship offers for Seattle land between September and December. Housing searches peak between January and April. That gap is where most people lose their good options — not because they were careless, but because June feels a long way off in November.

Here is the timeline that actually works, and the arithmetic worth doing before you commit.

The honest reason to act earlier than feels necessary

Seattle's intern housing supply is not elastic. The purpose-built options fill on a first-come basis and do not expand to meet a spring rush:

  • Company-arranged housing (Amazon and Microsoft both route interns through corporate providers; ABODA is the long-standing one here) — if it is in your offer, this is sorted for you, but you take what you are assigned
  • University summer housing — UW's housing office runs a summer intern programme with furnished rooms and a minimum stay of around 28 nights; Cornish Commons also sells summer beds. These are built for exactly your situation, and they fill early
  • Sublets — cheapest, least certain, and the supply only appears in spring
  • A room in a co-living building — more than a sublet, less than corporate housing, and the building still exists and is run by someone when something goes wrong at 11pm

The first two are the ones that run out. If you want either, deciding in February is late.

The timeline

When What to do
Offer lands (Sep–Dec) Ask HR one question: is housing provided, subsidised, or on me? The answer changes everything downstream. Do not assume.
Dec–Jan If housing is on you: work out your commute constraint first (below). Shortlist 3–4 options. Ask about dates — most listings assume a year, not twelve weeks.
Jan–Feb Decide. This is the window where you still have choice. University summer housing and corporate overflow start filling now.
Feb–Mar Confirm and hold. Get the hold terms in writing.
Apr–May Logistics: arrival date, keys, what is in the room and what is not.
Jun Arrive.

If you are reading this in October with an offer in hand, you are early — which is the good position to be in, and unusual.

Work out the commute constraint before you shortlist

In Seattle this decides more than the room does, because the city is cut up by water and hills rather than laid out on a grid.

South Lake Union — where a large share of the city's tech employment sits. Immediately south-east of Queen Anne, no bridge in the way, short and predictable. Most people making this commute do not keep a car.

Downtown — around ten minutes by car from Queen Anne, well served by bus.

Redmond — across Lake Washington, which means the 520 bridge, twice a day. There is one saving grace and it is counterintuitive: you would be reverse-commuting. The heavy morning flow is westbound into Seattle, so eastbound moves against the queue. It is still a bridge crossing with no alternative route when something goes wrong, and worth being honest with yourself about for twelve weeks.

The mistake to avoid is optimising for rent and inheriting a commute. Forty-five minutes each way over twelve weeks is roughly ninety hours — more than two full working weeks of your time in Seattle spent travelling. Against that, the monthly difference between a cheap room far out and a close one is usually small.

The arithmetic nobody runs

If you are comparing a Seattle offer against New York or California, do not compare the headline salary.

Washington has no state income tax. In New York you would pay state and city income tax on top of federal; in California, state income tax. Here, neither.

On an intern salary that is not a rounding error, and it lands in your account rather than in a calculation you never see. Run the comparison on take-home against rent, not gross against rent. Seattle rents look high until you do.

Then subtract setup. An empty room costs several hundred dollars and a weekend to make liveable — bed, desk, chair, bedding — and you will not recover that in twelve weeks. A furnished all-in room removes the line entirely.
