Modern living in Queen Anne

Emerson Quarters is a renovated apartment building at 608 W Emerson St in Seattle's Queen Anne neighborhood. Each unit features private bedrooms, a shared kitchen, and bathroom - all fully furnished and move-in ready.

Updated with modern finishes, the building offers balconies with neighborhood views, on-site laundry, and parking. Designed for students and young professionals who want a comfortable, affordable home base in one of Seattle's most desirable neighborhoods.
