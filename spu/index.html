Questions

Emerson Quarters is about a 3-minute walk from Seattle Pacific University's campus.

Your rent covers a fully furnished private bedroom with a bed, desk, chair, TV, and storage. Utilities (water, electricity, heat, garbage) and high-speed WiFi are all included. You share the kitchen and bathroom with your roommates in the unit.

You sign an individual lease for your private bedroom. You're only responsible for your room's rent, not the entire unit. We handle roommate matching and each person has their own lease agreement.

Standard leases run 6 or 12 months. Shorter terms may be available depending on availability. Contact us to discuss your needs.
